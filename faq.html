Which areas do you cover?+

We serve customers across Singapore. Share your postal code and what you need help with when requesting a quote.

Can I ask for a quote first?+

Yes. Use the quote form, WhatsApp or phone to share the basics before discussing the work.

What information should I include?+

The service you need, a short description of the issue and your postal code are a good starting point. Photos can be helpful too.

Do you support commercial spaces?+

Yes, we accept both residential and commercial enquiries. Tell us about the space and the work involved.

What are your operating hours?+

We operate Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. For urgent electrical faults we offer 24/7 support — please call first.

How fast will you respond?+

Usually within 1 business hour during operating hours (Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM). For urgent electrical faults, call first — we offer 24/7 support.

How much will the work cost?+

Every job is quoted individually and confirmed with you before work begins. Each service page shows guide prices (for example, troubleshooting from $80) so you know what to expect.

Do you handle emergencies?+

For urgent electrical faults we offer 24/7 support — call +65 9044 6055 first. Availability depends on the situation and current schedule.

Which services do you offer?+

Electrical, CCTV & network, aircon, handyman, plumbing and painting — plus waterproofing, vinyl flooring, metalwork and shelters. See the services page for the full list.

How do I contact HandyTech?+

Call +65 9044 6055, email hello@handytech.sg, or send a WhatsApp message from the contact page.

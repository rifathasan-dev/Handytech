Common questions

4 things people ask us most

How much does a CCTV installation cost?+

A full installation starts from $280. Troubleshooting and camera faults from $80, NVR/DVR setup from $150, data cabling from $120, and network points from $100.

Do my cameras work on my phone?+

Yes — mobile viewing is set up as part of the installation so you can check feeds and recordings away from home.

Can you work with cameras I already own?+

In most cases yes. Send the brand and model with a photo and we will confirm what is supported before quoting.

How long does installation take?+

It depends on the number of cameras and cabling routes. We confirm timing with you before the appointment so there are no surprises.

Common questions

4 things people ask us most

Do you handle urgent electrical faults?+

Yes. For urgent faults we offer 24/7 support — call +65 9044 6055 first so we can plan the fastest safe response.

How much does a visit cost?+

Troubleshooting starts from $80. Lighting from $80, power points from $90, DB and circuit work from $120, and wiring or rewiring from $180. Your quote is confirmed before work starts.

Will you restore power to the whole unit?+

If the fault is within the home's own wiring, points or DB, yes. If the supply itself is cut upstream by SP Group, that is outside our scope and we will point you to the right party.

Do I need to be home during the work?+

Not always. For occupied homes and workplaces we can agree access windows in advance and work respectfully around your routine.
